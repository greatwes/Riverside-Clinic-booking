Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Select “Book an appointment” or any doctor's “Book with” button, choose a doctor, pick an open date and time, then enter your name and email. You'll get a confirmation with a booking ID right away, no account needed.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Yes. Look yourself up by email under “Your bookings” and cancel from there, or use the cancel button on your confirmation screen right after booking. Cancelling frees the slot for someone else.

What if someone else books the same slot first?

Only one person can ever hold a given slot. If it's taken the moment you submit, we'll tell you right away and refresh the list so you can pick another time.

Why was my booking attempt rate limited?

To keep the system fair for everyone, each email address can attempt a booking up to 5 times per minute. If you hit that limit, just wait a few seconds and try again.

I submitted twice by accident. Was I double booked?

No. Retrying the same booking (for example after a slow connection) safely returns your original confirmation instead of creating a second appointment.

Still need help?

Jump back in whenever you're ready. Your slot list stays up to date.
